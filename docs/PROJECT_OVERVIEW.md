# Project Overview

## What is Stockox?
Stockox is an AI-Powered Multi-Agent Investment Intelligence Platform. It functions as an advanced financial operating system, combining the rich data analysis of a Bloomberg Terminal with the advanced reasoning capabilities of coordinated AI models, akin to a decentralized hedge fund's analytical division.

## Why it Exists
Retail investors face a significant information asymmetry compared to institutional investors. While raw data is accessible, synthesizing millions of data points—technical indicators, news sentiment, macro-economic factors, and fundamental metrics—into actionable intelligence is extremely difficult for a single person. Stockox bridges this gap by delegating these tasks to specialized AI agents.

## Core Vision
The core vision of Stockox is to automate the analytical heavy lifting in investment research. Instead of merely presenting charts and data feeds, Stockox interprets the data, debates its implications through multiple expert lenses, and provides high-confidence recommendations with fully transparent reasoning.

## The AI Committee Concept
Our key differentiator is the **AI Committee**. Rather than relying on a single, generalized AI prompt which can hallucinate or miss nuance, Stockox orchestrates a team of specialized AI personas:
- **Research Agent**: Focuses on fundamentals, financial health, and valuation.
- **Technical Agent**: Analyzes chart patterns, moving averages, momentum, and volume.
- **News Agent**: Interprets market sentiment, breaking news, and social signals.
- **Risk Agent**: Evaluates volatility, downside potential, portfolio exposure, and macro risks.
- **Committee Agent**: Aggregates the findings from all other agents, weights their votes, and synthesizes a final recommendation (e.g., BUY, HOLD, SELL) with an associated confidence score.

## Target Users
- **Active Retail Investors**: Looking for second opinions and deep-dive analysis without spending hours manually reading SEC filings.
- **Day / Swing Traders**: Utilizing the Technical and News agents for rapid sentiment and momentum checks.
- **Fintech Enthusiasts**: Developers and early adopters interested in the intersection of AI, Web3 (Band Protocol), and traditional finance.

## Use Cases
1. **Automated Due Diligence**: A user enters a ticker (e.g., "NVDA") and immediately receives a comprehensive 4-part agent report summarizing why the stock is moving.
2. **Portfolio Risk Assessment**: Evaluating current holdings against market volatility to determine if cash balances should be adjusted.
3. **Sentiment Tracking**: Quick checks on breaking news impacts without needing to manually sift through financial news websites.

## Business Value
- **Time Savings**: Condenses 4-6 hours of manual equity research into seconds.
- **Reduced Emotional Bias**: Agent-driven decisions rely on data and predefined analytical frameworks rather than FOMO or panic.
- **Enterprise-Grade Architecture**: Built on robust, scalable technologies (Next.js, Go, Supabase) ensuring high availability and secure data management.

## Market Opportunity
With the explosion of interest in generative AI and autonomous agents, there is a massive market opportunity to apply these technologies vertically within the wealth management and retail trading sectors. Stockox positions itself at the forefront of this movement by moving beyond "chatbots for finance" into structured, systematic "AI committees."
